## Verified Mozilla shutdown timeline

- **Announced:** May 22, 2025
- **Shut down:** July 8, 2025
- **Final data deletion:** November 12, 2025 (extended from October 8)

### Mozilla blog — https://blog.mozilla.org/en/mozilla/building-whats-next/

> "Pocket has helped millions save articles and discover stories worth reading."

> "the way people save and consume content on the web has evolved, so we're channeling our resources into projects that better match browsing habits today."

### Mozilla support — https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/future-of-pocket

> "We made the difficult decision to shut down Pocket on July 8, 2025."

> "As of November 12, 2025, user data export has been disabled, and all user data has been queued for permanent deletion."

**Subscription handling:** auto-canceled July 8, 2025; prorated refunds for annual plans.

## Killer narrative beats unlocked

1. **Mozilla's actual reason** is a perfect setup for our positioning. They said "the way people save and consume content has evolved." Punchline: yes — most of what people save now is Twitter threads.
2. **The al_borland HN quote** ("I never actually read things I save") is *literally* Totem's soul.md problem statement, captured in a stranger's voice.
3. **The "graveyard" framing** from the Medium piece is reusable as a section header.
4. **enzovarela's "write-only database"** is the perfect status quo description.
5. **Omnivore's death (Nov 2024)** plus Pocket's death (July 2025) is the **trust angle**: cloud-hosted read-later tools keep disappearing. Local-first (Totem) is the answer.
