What changed in the thinking

The redesign is a synthesis of six independent card concepts, adversarially judged, then red-teamed for voice/honesty and feasibility/accessibility. The spine is the top-scored Calm-Hero (the resting card must be effortless and identical all day), grafted with Recede & Return (Focus is a first-class verb) and Done-is-Enough (read and release are two doors to the same progress, encoded in the layout, not the copy). Three corrections were made over the raw synthesis: release routes through the real setIntent('reference') / snooze() calls (no fabricated reason that doesn't exist in code); Focus Mode blurs the card, not the surroundings (matching CONCEPTS.md, your instruction, and the focus-lens evidence — the synthesis had inverted it); and List + nav stay focusable for keyboard/AT discoverability.

The two load-bearing decisions (designed as questions, not beliefs)

Gauge direction weakShips: fill-by-item, neutral color, "2 of 5" — the framing that cannot read as a countdown. Why not drain: "minutes remaining draining to empty" rests on one un-replicated lab study (Koo & Fishbach 2012) whose sign can flip — it motivates finishers but pressures the ambivalent majority who dominate a 40× glance. A/B plan: remaining-focus is tested only inside the wind-down window, measured on % handled with real-read share as a hard cannibalization guard, guardrails holding veto.
"Not for me" release no direct evidenceThe mechanic the whole metric leans on, and the least grounded. Built for dignity + testability: same-size secondary button left of Read, undo ("Set aside. Undo" — matching the real 'reference' fate, not a dishonest "Released"), wired to setIntent('reference'). #1 A/B — present vs absent, watching % handled up without real-read share down, the guard live at launch.

Red-team fixes folded in

The voice/honesty critic flagged seven places the draft drifted into hype, false promises, or claims the pixels didn't deliver. All applied:

  1. Partial resume: "Pick up where you left off — 3 to go." → "Where you left off — 3 to go." (cut the productivity-app pep preamble; state the fact).
  2. Done subline: "Nothing waiting. A fresh set tomorrow." → "Nothing waiting." (drop a forward-promise the product can't guarantee on a no-bookmarks day).
  3. Wind-down: stop stacking four warming layers as a bundle → default to the minimum honest change; accent-tint / dusk / breathing each ship off by default behind separate flags.
  4. Undo verb: "Released. Undo" → "Set aside. Undo" (the post is filed for reference, not discarded — the word must match the real data fate).
  5. Wind-down setup: "…I'll finish today's read." → "…I'll sit with today's read." (frame the if-then around the act, not a completion the UI then scores you against).
  6. Equal-weight honesty: stop calling Read/Not-for-me "equal weight." Named honestly: Read is the visual primary; Not for me is a real, same-size secondary — and filled-Read-vs-equal-doors becomes an explicit release-A/B arm.
  7. Rhythm: "You read on 12 of the last 30 days." → "You've read on 12 days this month." (drop the denominator so there's no 12/30 ratio to fail against).

Feasibility & accessibility — verified against the real tokens

PermissionsEvery mechanic is pure client-side render/state. Zero new Chrome permissions. Wind-down is just a comparison against the already-running 30s now tick — no idle/alarms.
Real wiringGauge = CSS width on the existing meter slot. Release = setIntent('reference')/snooze() + undoHandled (all exist in useTodayQueue). Focus = a boolean + CSS filter. All map to NewTabHome.tsx:404–475.
Reduced-motionNeeds a new card-scoped rule (the existing exception only covers .totem-popover): done exhale → instant crossfade, breathing → off, dusk → static tint, focus blur → instant. Tap feedback stays <100ms.
No color-only signalCount "2 of 5" always accompanies the gauge; both actions carry text labels (WCAG 1.4.1). ✓
Keyboard / ATList + Prev/Next stay focusable toolbar buttons (visually recessive, not removed) so discoverability survives. Focus Mode restores on focus-enter so nobody operates a blurred control.
Open riskDusk-palette contrast is a promise, not yet proven — dimming lightness + warming hue could push body text under 4.5:1, especially dark mode. Must be measured before that layer ships.

What the redesign removes

The two competing meters (segmented step bar + continuous progress bar) collapse into one neutral hairline.
Accent color leaves the gauge by day — it now appears in exactly one resting place.
"Read" stops being the only positive action — "Not for me" joins it, advancing the same count.
Any standalone minutes gauge — minutes stay a per-post fact in the byline.
Any session/browser-time countdown — impossible without permissions and dishonest.
The instant done-swap → one quiet ~700ms exhale; no celebration beyond it, ever.
Any "you didn't finish" nag from a partial set.
The live "N reading now" co-presence chip — tested and declined (no privacy-safe count; the pressure citation was refuted).

Open decisions — these are yours

  1. Release semantics: should "Not for me" map to setIntent('reference') (set aside, keep for future lookup) or snooze() (defer to tomorrow)? I lean reference — it's truer to "not for me today" and matches the "Set aside" copy — but the long-term fate of the post is your call.
  2. Read button: keep Read filled-coral as the control (and test demoting it as an arm), or demote it to a quiet sibling from day one? I kept it filled to avoid stacking two unproven bets in the control.
  3. Scope & sequence: ship the calm default card + release + Focus first (it works fully with no wind-down), and add the net-new wind-down subsystem as a fast-follow? Or build activation now?
  4. Lowest-value layers: Reading Rhythm, dusk, and breathing are the most skippable. In the initial build at all, or deferred until the core card + release + Focus are validated?
