## Verbatim quotes from official X sources

### help.x.com — Bookmarks page
URL: https://help.x.com/en/using-x/bookmarks

> "Bookmarks lets you save posts in a timeline for easy, quick access at any time."
>
> "Tap the Bookmark icon to save a post. You will be prompted that the post has been Bookmarked and will be given the option to view all of your saved Bookmarks."
>
> **"Bookmarks are private and are only viewable to you within your X account."**

### @TwitterSupport announcement (March 16, 2023) — public bookmark counts

> **"We love Bookmarks for saving Tweets to revisit later. Starting today on iOS, you'll now see the total number of times a Tweet has been bookmarked on Tweet details."**

X also explicitly stated they would:
> **"never display which accounts have added a Tweet to their Bookmarks."**

### Elon Musk's "quiet like" framing — January 20, 2023
Quoted in Social Media Today (https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/twitter-adds-tweet-bookmark-count-to-details-view/645268/):

> "Yes, that will be added in an upcoming release. Also, if your tweet is bookmarked, it will be treated as a 'quiet like' and increment your likes counter."

**Note:** the "quiet like" / increment-likes part NEVER SHIPPED. Use this for narrative tension — Musk floated it, X dialed back to count-only.

### @XEng — Likes go private (June 11, 2024)
> "This week we're making Likes private for everyone to better protect your privacy."

### Haofei Wang, Director of Engineering, X — May 2024 preview
> "Public likes are incentivizing the wrong behavior. For example, many people feel discouraged from liking content that might be 'edgy' in fear of retaliation from trolls, or to protect their public image. Soon you'll be able to like without worrying who might see it."
