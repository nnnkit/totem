Markdown export audit

2026-10-18 · Tested against 60 real bookmarks and 2 TweetDetail payloads captured from a logged-in X session, then a second pass over 271 posts (search, home and Bookmarks timelines plus 7 TweetDetail payloads covering polls, player and unified cards, Articles with embedded posts, replies and a self-thread), run through the real parser and exporter (parseBookmarkPagePayload, then resolveReaderExportArticle, then articleToMarkdown).

Root cause

Plain tweets and threads were first turned into markdown-ish text. That text was then fed back through the article pipeline, which ran heading detection, paragraph re-flow, escaping and link regexes over it a second time. Most of the visible breakage came from this second pass. Tweets now get a dedicated renderer (src/lib/export/tweet-markdown.ts) that works from structured data and skips the article heuristics.

Still not supported

  • Article image galleries and nested list depth: not implemented. 27 real Articles were scanned. None had an atomic block with several entities, a MEDIA entity with several mediaItems, or a depth above 0. Consecutive single-image blocks already export every image.
  • App-install cards: none were found (App Store links come back as plain summary cards). Unified cards with an app destination are skipped, since there is no shape to follow.
  • poll2choice_text_only wasn't captured. The parser reads any choiceN_label set, so it should work, but it is untested on real data.
  • Embedded posts in Articles link out only. The post itself isn't in the Article payload, so there is no author or text.
  • Replies to several people show only the first handle ("Replying to @a"), because the parser keeps in_reply_to_screen_name.
  • PDF export still goes through the old plain-text path.
  • Posts already saved: bold/italic, polls, cards and reply prefixes appear only once the post is synced or its detail is fetched again. The stored rows were parsed by the old parser.
