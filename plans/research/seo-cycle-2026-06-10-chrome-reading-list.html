Outcome

Intent

Users trying to find, use, or understand Chrome Reading List, plus readers comparing built-in read-later tools.

Cluster

Decision

Publish a concise explainer that answers the literal how-to, then pivots to the save-vs-read thesis. The winning angle is not "Chrome Reading List is bad." It is "Chrome Reading List is a quiet side-panel list, so it fails when the user needs a return path."
