01 · Executive Summary

Executive Summary

Totem should not copy the "hundreds of pages" version of long-tail SEO. The current data points to a tighter sprint: five editorial pages and three branded comparison pages, backed by exact X bookmark pain and product proof.

The Decision

  • Build next: clear/delete bookmarks, Twitter bookmark managers, Chrome Reading List, new-tab page intent, new-tab extensions, and three competitor pages.
  • Defer: NotebookLM, Obsidian, AI-copy, generic bookmark manager, and X reader keywords until Search Console or community distribution proves demand.
  • Use CWS for CWS-shaped intent: export, saver, and exact extension searches are often store-result SERPs. Blog pages support them, but the listing is the ranking surface.
Core thesis
The next SEO win is not more pages. It is better page-to-intent fit: exact X bookmark problems, honest comparisons, and a repeated claim that Totem owns the new-tab reading queue.

The SERPs for missing bookmarks are not clean. They mix app bugs, deleted/protected posts, API limits, and UI confusion. Totem's advantage is explaining those causes separately, then showing the local backup/read queue as the prevention layer.

Positioning Rules

  1. Do not claim Totem is the best universal bookmark manager. It is for X bookmarks as a reading queue.
  2. Do not split every 10-volume phrase into its own page. Fold tails into stronger canonical pages.
  3. Use the product truth repeatedly: Twitter bookmarks show up on every new tab, ready to read, locally.
